Terms & Privacy

Terms of use

Landier is a marketplace that connects people buying and selling land in Kenya. Landier is not a party to any sale and does not hold money on anyone's behalf.

Sellers must own the land they list, or be authorised to sell it, and must describe it honestly. Listings are reviewed before they appear, but a review is not a legal guarantee of ownership.

Before paying any money, buyers should visit the land, do an official search at the Ministry of Lands (Ardhisasa), confirm the seller's ID matches the title, and use a registered advocate for the sale agreement.

We may remove listings or accounts that are fraudulent, misleading, or abusive.

Privacy

We store your name, email, and phone number to run your account. Your name and phone number are shown on land you list so buyers can contact you. Messages are visible only to you and the other person in the chat, and to administrators investigating reported fraud.

Your location is used only on your device to centre the map; it is not stored. You can delete your account and listings at any time from Settings.
